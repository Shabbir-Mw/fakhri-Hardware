Who We Are

Fakhri Hardware & Plywood is a trusted name in Ujjain for all your plywood, laminate, and furniture fitting needs. Located on Sanwer Road in Nana Kheda, we have been serving contractors, carpenters, interior designers, and home owners across Ujjain with quality products at the best prices.

Whether you're building new furniture, renovating your home, or working on a large construction project — we have everything you need under one roof. Our wide selection of plywood grades, laminate finishes, and hardware fittings ensures that you find exactly what you're looking for.

We believe in honest dealing, quality products, and building long-term relationships with our customers. Visit our shop and experience the difference of working with a team that truly cares about your project.
